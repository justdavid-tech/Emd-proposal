    01

    The problem

    A buyer wants to know if EMD&C Global can be trusted with their money. Today they call, ask around or wait for photos on WhatsApp. That is slower for them, slower for you, and it quietly costs sales every month.

    Stock cannot be seen

    Cars, properties and appliances are known only to people who already know you. Everyone else buys from whoever shows up first online.

    Trust is judged before you are called

    Serious buyers look a company up before they visit. A thin or missing website can lose the sale before a conversation starts.

    Every request means resending photos

    Prices, pictures and documents go out one by one on WhatsApp and email. It is slow, inconsistent and eats your team’s day.

    Enquiries are not tracked

    Calls, messages and referrals arrive in different places. Leads get lost and nobody knows which business brings the best customers.

    Viewings take too many calls

    Arranging a test drive, an inspection or a site visit takes several calls. Every delay is a chance for the buyer to go elsewhere.

    New stock stays invisible

    Fresh arrivals and new listings prove you are active, but without a place to show them, customers assume you are quiet.

    Security patches

    Enquiries and your admin login stay protected as new risks appear.

    Third party services

    WhatsApp tools, maps and forms update on their own schedule, and we keep up.

    Backups and recovery

    Listings, photos and enquiries are backed up so nothing is lost.

    10

    Our honest recommendation

    Three businesses sell to three very different customers. A buyer shopping for a car is not the same person looking for a house or a generator. So we advise a separate website for each business, with its own focus, its own address and its own dashboard.

    Each customer finds you faster

    A focused site ranks better on Google for its own searches, and visitors are never lost in a menu of unrelated things.

    Each business runs on its own

    Separate dashboards and separate staff access mean the car team never touches property listings, and a problem in one never slows another.

    Each brand can grow freely

    You can sell, partner or expand any business later without untangling a shared website.

    Start small, add more

    You can begin with one website and add the others when you are ready.
